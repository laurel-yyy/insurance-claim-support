# General insurance FAQ (fictional company policy for this demo)

This page is general information. It is not tied to any account and never describes a specific claim.

## What is an EOB?
An Explanation of Benefits (EOB) is a statement sent after a claim is processed. It shows what the provider
billed, what the plan allowed, what the plan paid, and what the member may owe. An EOB is not a bill.

## Deductible, copay and coinsurance
- **Deductible**: the amount a member pays for covered services each plan year before the plan starts paying.
- **Copay**: a fixed amount paid for a covered service, such as an office visit.
- **Coinsurance**: a percentage of the allowed amount the member pays after the deductible is met.

## In-network and out-of-network
In-network providers have agreed rates with the plan, so members usually pay less. Out-of-network care may cost
more or may not be covered, depending on the plan.

## What does an appeal mean?
An appeal is a formal request to review a denied claim again. It usually includes the missing or corrected
documents and must be filed before the appeal deadline. Filing a formal appeal isn't available in this chat; a
member of the claims team can help with it.

## How are documents submitted?
The member portal or the claim upload link is the fastest way. If online upload isn't possible, the support team
can arrange fax or mail.
